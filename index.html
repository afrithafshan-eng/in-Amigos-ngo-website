WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization founded in 2020 with the goal of creating positive social change.

The organization works through community-based initiatives focusing on education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental conservation and skill development.

Through volunteers and social initiatives, InAmigos aims to empower communities and create meaningful opportunities for people in need.

Our Mission

To bring people together and create meaningful social impact through education, empowerment, compassion and community participation.
